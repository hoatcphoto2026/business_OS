# Architecture Decisions (P1-3 / Item 5)

## Workflow — primary choice
**Primary:** `src/modules/workflow` (B1 Workflow Definition Engine)
- Use for: configurable approval/process definitions, versioning, execute/retry
- Tables: `workflow_definitions`, `workflow_nodes`, `workflow_transitions`, `workflow_executions`

**Secondary:** `src/modules/automation-workflow`
- Use for: event-driven action handlers (SEND_EMAIL, ACTIVATE_SUBSCRIPTION, …)
- Do not use for new approval flows

## Invoice — primary choice
**Primary:** `billing_invoices` (Billing module)
- Source of truth for subscription billing, tax, coupon, payment linkage

**Automation helper:** `auto_invoices`
- Generated by Auto Invoice Engine; should be reconciled into `billing_invoices` for official records

**Sales:** `invoices` (sales-orders)
- Business/sales invoices only; not subscription billing
